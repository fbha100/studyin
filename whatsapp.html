What Studi never handles alone

The value of an assistant like this is decided by how fast it gives up. These triggers bypass every other rule and route straight to a counsellor.

Visa refusal or appeal

Legally consequential and emotionally loaded. Wrong information here can cost a student an intake.

IMMEDIATE HANDOFF

Money and payments

Deposits, refunds, agent fees, loan letters. Studi never quotes a figure or takes a payment instruction.

IMMEDIATE HANDOFF

Distress or pressure

Family pressure, anxiety, anything sounding like a crisis. A human replies, not a script.

IMMEDIATE HANDOFF

Complaints and disputes

Anything about StudyIn's own service goes to a manager, never to the assistant that may have caused it.

IMMEDIATE HANDOFF

How students get into the thread

The channel only works if it's continuous with everything else. Studi on the website and Studi on WhatsApp are the same character, mid-conversation.

From the website

The Studi dock offers "Continue on WhatsApp" once a student has picked a destination or run a course search. The context travels with them — Studi opens already knowing what they were looking at.

HIGHEST INTENT

Click-to-WhatsApp ads

Meta and Instagram ads that open the thread directly instead of a landing page. The opening message names the ad's destination, so there's no cold start.

PAID ACQUISITION

QR at fairs and branches

A printed Studi QR on every counsellor's desk and fair stand. Scanning it attaches the branch, so follow-up comes from someone the student actually met.

OFFLINE TO ONLINE

How you'd know it's working

Deflection rate is the wrong headline metric — it rewards a bot for refusing to escalate. These are the numbers that track whether students are actually better off.

Time to first useful answer

Not time to first reply. An instant "how can I help?" isn't an answer.

TARGET: UNDER 30s

Handoff acceptance

How often a student stays engaged after a counsellor joins. Measures handoff quality, not volume.

TARGET: 80%+

Document completion

Share of reminded students who submit within 72 hours, against an unreminded control group.

THE REAL PAYOFF

Opt-out rate

The honest early-warning signal. Rising opt-outs mean the reminders became broadcasts.
